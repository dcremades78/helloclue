01 · What we found

What we saw when we looked at Clue on social.

Before writing anything, we looked at how people talk about Clue and period tracking apps today. Four things stood out.

150M+ installs

A science-led app with 1M+ paid subscribers.

Clue has passed 150M total installs and reached 1M+ Clue Plus subscribers in early 2026. Rated 4.8★ on the App Store, women-led and built in Berlin. The product is strong. The creator layer is where it can grow.

Privacy matters

Post-Dobbs, data trust is a deciding factor.

Clue stores data under EU law and has publicly stated it will never share health data with any authority. After Roe v. Wade, this is a real selling point that competitors based in the US cannot match.

People search for it

TikTok is a search engine for cycle content.

Searches like "best period tracker app" and "cycle syncing app" drive discovery on TikTok. OBGYNs and hormone creators already talk about cycle tracking. Placing Clue inside that conversation drives trials long after a video is posted.

Cycle syncing is trending

The wellness trend that fits Clue perfectly.

Cycle syncing content is growing fast on TikTok and Instagram. Creators who teach women to align workouts, food and routines with their cycle need a tracking app. Clue is the obvious recommendation.

Sources: Business of Apps on installs and revenue; Clue's website on 1M+ subscribers and privacy policy; App Store and Google Play ratings; TikTok search pages on period tracking. Checked November 2026.

04 · A wildcard worth testing

A creator format nobody expects from a period app.

Next to the shortlist, we'd test one bold format in the first wave. A few creators, measured like the rest. If it works, it becomes a channel of its own.

Wildcard 1

"My Body, My Data" Challenge

A TikTok challenge where creators show one thing they took control of about their health data. Starts with deleting an app that sells their data, ends with opening Clue. Privacy as empowerment, not as fear. Built for shares and duets.

"My body, my data. Step 1: delete the app that sells it. Step 2: meet the one that doesn't."
#mybodymydata · #periodtracker · #dataprivacy
How we'd test it: two or three creators inside the first wave, each with their own link, so you can compare cost per trial against the main shortlist. We'd scout these creators after our call.
